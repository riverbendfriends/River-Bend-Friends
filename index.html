Riverbend Friends

Nursery Rhymes & Kids Songs

Welcome to Riverbend, a sunny riverside village where Ollie the little otter, his family and his friends sing, play and learn together.

Riverbend Friends is a YouTube channel with gentle, original animated songs for toddlers and preschoolers, ages 1 to 4.

All videos are created with the help of AI tools and are reviewed and approved by a person before they are published.
