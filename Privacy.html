Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

About this app

"Riverbend Friends Uploader" is a private tool used only by the owner of the Riverbend Friends YouTube channel. It runs on the owner's own computer and uploads the channel's own videos to YouTube, including the title, description, tags, thumbnail and audience settings.

Use of YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data we collect

This app does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data of viewers or other YouTube users. It only accesses the channel owner's own YouTube account, with the owner's permission, in order to upload videos to that channel.

Storage of credentials

The authorization token is stored only locally on the owner's computer and is never shared with third parties. Access can be revoked at any time via the Google security settings page.

Children's privacy

The Riverbend Friends channel publishes content made for children. This app does not collect any information from children or from any viewers.
